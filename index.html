    Why it works

    Built for the way a survey is actually done

    🚢

    Your ship, your tables

    Each app is built with one vessel's hydrostatic, tank and trim tables. Just pick the drafts — the lookups and interpolation are done for you.

    🔗

    Surveys that feed each other

    Initial, Int1, Int2 and Final surveys flow straight into Sounding and Trimming with one tap — no re-typing.

    📄

    One-tap PDF report

    Print or save the survey as a clean PDF report, and import / export your data to continue on another device.

    📡

    Works offline

    Activate once online; after that the calculations run on your device — no signal needed on the bridge or at anchor.

    🔒

    Ship data stays protected

    Ship files are encrypted and only open with a valid licence, so your vessel's data isn't passed around.

    🌗

    Dark or light, desktop or phone

    A night-friendly dark theme for the bridge, a light one for the office, and a layout that fits a phone screen.

    On your phone too

    Take it to the draft marks

    The Android version uses the same calculations and the same layout, scaled for a phone.

    • Read the marks, type the drafts, see the cargo weight on the spot
    • Export the data and finish the report on the PC later

    How it works

    From drafts to report in three steps

    Enter the readings

    Six draft marks, density and the ship's constant, fuel, water and ballast.

    Press Compute

    Heel, trim, hog/sag, corrected drafts, displacement and cargo weight appear instantly.

    Print the report

    Compare with the B/L figure, then save or print a PDF for the file.
